How it works

Every claim goes through the same pipeline.

  1. Read the billAn LLM extracts the patient, diagnosis and amount.
  2. Check the rulesAmount, name match and the policy’s exclusion list.
  3. Search the handbookFAISS finds the most relevant policy pages (RAG).
  4. Decide and explainGuardrails give a verdict and a cited report.
